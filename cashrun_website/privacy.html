Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Cash Run ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personal information (like your name, email, or phone number). However, our App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

The information collected by third-party services may include:

  • Device Information (model, OS version, unique device identifiers).
  • IP Address and approximate location data.
  • Usage Data (interaction with ads, crash reports, performance data).
  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android).

2. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services in our App:

  • Google AdMob: To serve advertisements. AdMob may use cookies and unique device identifiers to serve personalized ads based on your interests.
  • Google Firebase: For analytics, crash reporting, and database services to save your game progress.

You can learn more about how Google uses your data here: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. How We Use Information

The data collected by third-party services is used to:

  • Display personalized advertisements to you.
  • Monitor the performance and stability of the App (crash analytics).
  • Save your game progress (level and score) securely.

4. Your Choices

Opt-out of Targeted Advertising:

You can opt-out of personalized advertising by adjusting the settings on your device:

  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising or Tracking.
  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
